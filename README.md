# The Midnight Market

**The Midnight Market** is a Level 1–2 fantasy one-shot adventure featured in the _40 One Shot Adventures_ collection. It is set in a mysterious, supernatural bazaar that materializes only after sunset and vanishes completely with the first light of dawn, leaving behind only silence and empty streets.

  

## Plot Overview

The adventurers enter the Midnight Market to track down a notorious thief named Silla the Shadow, who has stolen a valuable artifact. To recover the item, the players must navigate narrow, misty alleyways, interact with eccentric vendors, avoid the watchful eye of the night watchman, and corner Silla before the sun rises.

  

## Main Characters (NPCs)

### Silla the Shadow

- **Race/Gender/Class:** Half-Orc, Female, Thief / Illusion Specialist
    
      
    
- **Combat Statistics:** Armor Class (AC) 14 | Hit Points (HP) 28
    
      
    
- **Role:** A legendary thief who stole the Chirano Overload Core from the Mayor's office, believing it to be a magical gem capable of breaking her curse. She hides in the market's deepest shadows, utilizing illusions, tripwires, and traps.
    
      
    

### Caldwin the Quick

- **Race/Gender/Class:** Elf, Male, Rogue Vendor
    
      
    
- **Combat Statistics:** Armor Class (AC) 13 | Hit Points (HP) 22
    
      
    
- **Role:** A cunning merchant trading in bizarre baubles, exotic potions, and unauthorized equipment. He barters clues or contraband in exchange for personal items of sentimental value rather than standard gold.
    
      
    

### Hessia Wraithcall

- **Race/Gender/Class:** Human, Female, Cursed Fortune-Teller / Oracle
    
      
    
- **Combat Statistics:** Armor Class (AC) 12 | Hit Points (HP) 24
    
      
    
- **Role:** Spiritually bound to the market, Hessia reads the currents of fate from behind a veiled table smelling of burnt herbs and black candles. She provides guidance if players solve her riddle:
    
      
    - _"I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. I have no body, but I come alive with wind."_ (Answer: **An echo**)
        
          
        

### Emmett, the Night Watchman

- **Race/Gender/Class:** Dwarf, Male, Market Watchman
    
      
    
- **Combat Statistics:** Armor Class (AC) 16 | Hit Points (HP) 35
    
      
    
- **Role:** A gruff, incorruptible watchman maintaining order within the market's magical borders. He can be convinced through bribery or roleplay to create a loud distraction in the central square, drawing away threats to allow covert entry.
    
      
    

## Flow of the Adventure

1. **The Entrance:** The party is guided through a dense alleyway mist smelling of incense and damp cobblestone into the heart of the market.
    
      
    
2. **Gathering Clues:** The players investigate Silla's trail by bartering with Caldwin or solving Hessia's riddle, which points toward "shadows within shadows" near the ruins of the old cathedral.
    
      
    
3. **The Watchman's Patrol:** Throughout their investigation, the party must evade or manage Emmett's security patrols.
    
      
    
4. **The Confrontation:** The party corners Silla in her dark hideout, where she defends herself with illusions and warns them of the artifact's dangerous energy.
    
      
    
5. **The Escape:** After securing the artifact, the players must exit before dawn as the market dissolves back into empty streets.
    
      
    

## Rewards of Valor

- **The Moon’s Mark (Tattoo):** A shimmering, magical tattoo awarded upon completion that grants a +1 bonus to Dexterity and advantage on underhanded dealings.
    
      
    

# West Marches Campaign Stuff: The Midnight Market

A custom Foundry VTT module designed for campaign assets, compendiums, and configurations for **The Midnight Market** adventure.

  

## Features

- Custom compendium packs for campaign entities (Actors, Items, Journals, and Scenes).
    
      
    
- Tailored for Foundry VTT v14 and D&D 5e.
    
      
    

## Installation

1. Clone or download this repository into your Foundry VTT user data directory under `Data/modules/the-midnight-market`.
    
      
    
2. Ensure the folder name matches `the-midnight-market`.
    
      
    
3. Launch Foundry VTT, navigate to the **Add-on Modules** tab, and verify the module is detected.
    
      
    
4. Enable the module inside your active game world.
    
      
    

## Manifest URL

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serelith-Varn/the-midnight-market/main/module.json`

  

## 🔍 Usage

Once enabled:

  

- Go to the **Compendium** tab
    
      
    
- Look for `The Midnight Market` packs
    
      
    
- Drag and drop entities into your respective directories
