# The Midnight Market

The Midnight Market is a Level 1–2 fantasy one-shot adventure featured in the *40 One Shot Adventures* book. It is set in a mysterious, supernatural bazaar that only appears after sunset and completely vanishes with the first light of dawn, leaving nothing behind but silence and empty streets.

## Plot Overview
The adventurers enter the Midnight Market to track down a notorious thief named Silla the Shadow who has stolen a valuable artifact. To recover the artifact, the players must navigate the market's narrow, misty alleyways, deal with its eccentric vendors, avoid the watchful night watchman, and corner Silla before the sun rises.

## Main Characters (NPCs)

### Silla the Shadow (Half-Orc, Female, Thief) | AC 14 | HP 28
* **Role:** A highly skilled thief who stole the artifact specifically to break a curse. She hides in the market's deepest shadows, using illusions and traps to defend herself. If cornered, she warns the players about the artifact's dark curse.

### Caldwin the Quick (Elf, Male, Rogue Vendor) | AC 13 | HP 22
* **Role:** A cunning merchant trading in bizarre baubles. He knows many of the market's secrets and will barter vague clues about Silla's whereabouts if the players trade him a valuable personal item.

### Hessia Wraithcall (Human, Female, Cursed Fortune-Teller) | AC 12 | HP 24
* **Role:** Bound to the market, Hessia sits behind a veiled table smelling of burnt herbs, candles, and strange symbols. She offers cryptic guidance regarding Silla's last sighting in exchange for payment or if the players can solve her riddle:
  * *"I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. I have no body, but I come alive with wind."* (Answer: **An echo**)

### Emmett, the Night Watchman (Dwarf, Male, Watchman) | AC 16 | HP 35
* **Role:** A gruff, no-nonsense watchman who patrols the market to maintain order. He keeps a close eye on suspicious adventurers, especially near restricted stalls. While a bribe can keep him at bay, causing a public scene will immediately draw his wrath.

## Flow of the Adventure
1. **The Entrance:** The party is led through a dense alleyway mist smelling of incense and damp cobblestone by flickering lanterns into the heart of the market.
2. **Gathering Clues:** The players must search for Silla's trail. Pressuring Caldwin can accidentally reveal her escape route, while visiting Hessia's tent reveals she was last spotted near the fortune-teller's tent ("shadows within shadows").
3. **The Watchman's Shadow:** Throughout their investigation, they must dodge or bribe Emmett, who monitors restricted stalls.
4. **The Confrontation:** The party eventually corners Silla in her silent, dark hideout. She uses magic, traps, and illusions to escape if they try to use force.
5. **The Escape:** After securing the artifact, the players must leave before dawn, as the entire market vanishes with the first light of morning.

## Rewards of Valor
* **The Moon’s Mark (Tattoo):** A shimmering, magical tattoo awarded to the players that grants a +1 bonus to Dexterity and luck in underhanded dealings.
