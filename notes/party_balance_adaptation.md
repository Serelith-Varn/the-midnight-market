# Encounter Balance & Campaign Adaptation: Mixed Level Party (3x Lvl 3/4 & 2x Lvl 7)

## The Core Challenge
A party composed of three Level 3-4 characters and two Level 7 characters represents a **massive power disparity** in D&D 5e / Foundry systems. 
- **Level 7 PCs** possess multiattack, 4th-level spells (like *Dimension Door*, *Banishment*, or *Polymorph*), significantly higher hit point pools, and Tier 2 feat/class synergies.
- **Level 3-4 PCs** are still in Tier 1, prone to being downed by single critical hits from moderate threats, lacking reliable area-of-effect control, and restricted to 2nd-level spells.

If you balance encounters for Level 7s, the Level 3-4s will instantly die to collateral damage. If you balance for Level 3-4s, the Level 7s will trivialize every combat instantly.

---

## Strategic Adaptation Options

### 1. The "Sidekick & Mentor" Dynamic (Narrative Split)
* **Concept:** Formally split the party's roles in combat based on narrative weight. The Level 7 characters are legendary bodyguards, mentors, or supernatural patrons protecting the lower-level adventurers.
* **Execution:** 
  * Give the Level 7 characters high-priority objectives that don't just involve killing regular mooks (e.g., holding back a collapsing ceiling, protecting a civilian NPC, or engaging the elite boss while the lower-level PCs clear out lieutenants).
  * In combat, assign the Level 7s distinct hazards or legendary threats that demand their full attention, keeping them too busy to one-shot the encounters meant for the Level 3-4s.

### 2. Tier-Splitting Encounter Design (Asymmetric Encounters)
Instead of a single homogeneous mob, design encounters with two distinct tiers of threats:
* **The Vanguard (For Lvl 3-4s):** Minions, swarms, and mid-tier bruisers (CR 1/2 to CR 2) that the lower-level characters can engage in tactical skirmishes.
* **The Apex Threat (For Lvl 7s):** A solo elite, spellcaster, or heavy construct (CR 6 to CR 8) that immediately locks horns with the Level 7 PCs.
* **Foundry VTT Tip:** Use separate token groups and distinct initiative tracking or automated combat groups so the flow of battle remains manageable.

### 3. Level Equalization via Boons & Sidekick Levels (Mechanical Boost)
If you want a more unified party feel without altering base character sheets:
* **Give the Lvl 3-4s Powerful Magic Items:** Provide items with high-impact utility or defense (e.g., *Brooch of Shielding*, *Pearl of Power*, custom charms, or scrolls of *Revivify* and *Fireball*) to punch above their weight class.
* **Apply Tasha’s Sidekick Rules:** Elevate the Level 3-4 characters using Warrior, Expert, or Spellcaster sidekick class features so their action economy and survivability scale closer to Tier 2 without changing their core class identity.

---

## Foundry VTT Implementation Recommendations
1. **Encounter Token Scaling:** When dragging monsters onto scenes in Foundry, separate encounters into distinct threat tiers. Ensure lower-level PCs have access to healing potions and defensive macros.
2. **Automated Shielding & Reaction Macros:** Make sure modules like *Midi-QOL* or *JB2A animations* are configured so the Level 7s can easily use reactions (like *Shield*, *Counterspell*, or *Interception* fighting styles) to protect their vulnerable companions.
