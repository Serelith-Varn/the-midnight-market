# The Setup: Mayor Tanner's Mechanical Crackdown
When the party returns to Corwyn to check on their newly established underground rave, they find the village under an aggressive, high-tech lockdown.

Furious about the noise and the goblin alliance, Mayor Tanner has gone to extreme lengths. She hired some cheap mercenaries to delve into the deeper, ancient Chiran Halls of the catacombs and retrieve some of the dormant, black-iron Chiran guardian constructs (the "mechs"). Tanner has reprogrammed these soulless, life-draining automatons to serve as a "No-Fun Patrol."

Now, these mechanical sentries stalk the foggy streets of Corwyn, enforcing an absolute, sound-dampening curfew. Any citizen caught humming, carrying an instrument, or out past dusk is immediately detained by a silent, floating metal guardian.

## The Underground Resistance: Enter the Midnight Market
To bypass the mechanical guards, the party discovers that a thick, magical fog has rolled into Corwyn’s back alleys. Inside this mist, the legendary Midnight Market has temporarily manifested.

The market vendors—normally neutral—are absolutely furious. The Mayor's new patrol mechs are ruining their business, scanning their patrons, and threatening to confiscate their exotic, illegal inventory. A natural alliance is born between your party, the goblins, and the market crew.

## NPC Alliances & Their Roles

### 1. The Goblin Allies (Sewer Smugglers)
* **Chieftain Sklaar:** He has his goblins digging narrow bypass tunnels from the Catacomb Rave directly up into the basements of the Midnight Market stalls, allowing ravers to sneak past the mechs.
* **Hector (The Teen Tech Specialist):** The 13-year-old mild-mannered Hector is fascinated by the Chiran constructs. He tells the party that the mechs are controlled by an ancient Chiran Overdrive Core. If they can get the Core, he can help them "hack" the mechs to stop attacking and instead serve as massive, glowing disco-ball strobes for the rave!

### 2. The Midnight Market Crew (The Enablers)
* **Silla the Shadow (The Key to the Core):** Silla has recently slipped into town and stolen the exact Chiran Overdrive Core from Mayor Tanner's office, thinking it was a rare, cursed gemstone she could use to break her own curse. She is hiding in the deepest shadows of the market. The players must find her and convince her to hand it over (or help her break her curse) so Hector can use it to override the mechs.
* **Caldwin the Quick (The Supplier):** Caldwin is secretly fencing "contraband" rave supplies to the party. He can sell them sound-muffling blankets, glowing neon Chiran crystals, and makeshift shock-bombs that can temporarily stun the mechanical sentries.
* **Hessia Wraithcall (The Oracle):** Hessia's fortune-telling tent sits right on the border of the market and the real world. She can read the "threads of the machine" to give the players the exact patrol routes and blind spots of the mechanical guards.
* **Emmett (The Watchman with a Grudge):** Emmett, the gruff dwarven night watchman of the market, is deeply offended by the Mayor's constructs. "A machine don't know how to take a proper bribe, and it sure as hell don't know how to keep the peace with dignity!" He is willing to act as a distraction, using his heavy shield and warhammer to draw a mech's attention while the party sneaks past.

## The Mission Flow
1. **Infiltrate Corwyn:** The players must sneak into town past the mechanical sentries, utilizing Luca’s Misty Step or Vaelis’s elven stealth.
2. **Locate the Market:** They follow the scent of incense and damp cobblestones to find the Midnight Market hidden in the fog.
3. **The Deal with Silla:** They track down Silla the Shadow. To get the Overdrive Core, they have to either solve Hessia's riddle to locate her hiding spot or protect her from a surprise mech patrol that invades the market.
4. **The Hack:** With the Core secured, they deliver it to Hector in the catacombs while Emmett and Sklaar's goblins launch a loud, chaotic street brawl to distract the remaining mechs.
5. **The Grand Opening:** Once Hector hacks the constructs, the party leads a massive parade of ravers from the Midnight Market down into the catacombs. The session ends with the three giant black-iron mechs shooting harmless, colorful lasers into the cave ceiling while Luca and Luna Spes blast bardic music to a crowd of dancing goblins and market vendors.
